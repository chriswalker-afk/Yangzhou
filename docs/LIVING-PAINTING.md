# Living Painting Architecture — pass 4

## Why
The paintings used in PR #3 already had boats, people, and steam baked into one image. Drawing new animated actors on top would produce duplicated, frozen figures. This pass deliberately splits the starter scene into a **clean plate**, **foreground occlusion** and **movable sprites**.

## Assets generated from the approved painted panorama
Generated by `scripts/build_living_assets.py`, using only `assets/painted/starter-panorama.webp`:

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `assets/living/starter-clean.webp` | Background without the marked static people and boats; repaired ground and water |
| `assets/living/bridge-foreground.png` | Transparent bridge roofs, balustrades and piers used to hide moving boats behind the bridge |
| `assets/living/willow-foreground.png` | Transparent foliage occlusion for appropriate visual overlap |
| `assets/living/boat-upper.png` | Painted upper-canal boat, independent of the background |
| `assets/living/boat-lower.png` | Painted lower-canal boat (including passengers), independent of background |
| `assets/living/walker-{blue,rose,ochre}.png` | Separate painted resident cut-outs for moving along the bridge |
| `assets/living/cleanup-mask.png` | Reusable editable mask showing reconstructed portions of the source |
| `assets/living/manifest.json` | Pixel dimensions, layer order, mask count and caveats |

These files are committed to the repository; the game loads them as local static assets. The accompanying `cleanup-mask.png` is deliberately retained to support hand-correction of any overlooked figures or smeared scenery in later art reviews.

## Modular tile architecture (supersedes the old panorama renderer)

PR #4 now places **six independent, complete square painted masters** on a fixed 3 × 2 Slender West Lake grid. The live board does not draw the earlier 1200 × 800 clean plate or original panorama beneath six invisible cells. The older assets remain valuable as an illustration reference and as the origin for some extracted actor sprites.

- `tile-topology.js` owns the six master assignments, the 13 placeable archetypes, 90-degree logical rotation and board validation.
- `tile-system.js` paints every individual square on the game canvas, including starter tiles. It selects a full-square source and uses topology-aware painting for provisional road/water branches.
- `edge-art.js` standardises centre ports on **every** tile: stone street 44/220, navigable water 88/220 and landscape without a port. A six-unit feather avoids the former broad tinted seam stripes.
- The old 3 × 2 panorama and clean plate are not rendered in the live grid. They remain accessible as concept/gallery images.
- All ten surrounding positions can be occupied together; see `tests/tile-topology.cjs` and the [tile artwork review studio](../tile-review.html).
- The six master files are currently visually *distinct files*, not six fully bespoke finished paintings. Repeated source motifs, bridge masks and terrain inconsistencies remain visible and require an art pass before release.

## Current scene layering

1. Each tile draws its own **static square background artwork** and terrain connectors.
2. A demonstration animation layer places **independent moving boats and walkers** in world coordinates on the starter lake/bridge.
3. The gameplay UI adds selection guides, placement previews and camera controls.

The legacy `drawScene` function preserves a layered clean-plate demonstration, but the active board uses `drawActors`, not that original atlas renderer. The old bridge and willow PNGs are not yet integrated as reliable foreground masks on each square tile. Boats are currently hidden through the crossing region instead of passing behind a carefully authored per-tile mask. Restoring visually correct bridge/roof occlusion is a required production pass.

## How to inspect

- Open `index.html` to play using the true square-tile renderer.
- Choose **Explore artwork** to hide placement guides, and use City / Street / Detail to inspect source-painting fidelity.
- The **Clean plate / Living city** toggle hides or restores demo moving objects while preserving the square-tile backgrounds. It no longer switches to the 1200 × 800 clean background on the board.
- Open [tile-review.html](../tile-review.html) to compare the six starting squares and expand all ten neighbour positions simultaneously.
- The **Paintings** gallery contains the earlier panorama and landmark reference illustrations; it is not an atlas used as the gameplay background.

## Authoring contract for new tiles
Production art should **never paint moving people, boats, animals, water steam, smoke or floating leaves into the base tile**. A future tile can specify:
- `background.webp`: water/ground/architecture with no movable subjects.
- `occluders/*.png`: nonmoving bridge/roof/tree elements, with transparent alpha.
- `actors/*.png`: painterly transparent sprites; a sprite can itself contain passengers if the whole sprite moves together.
- `anchors.json`: valid footpaths and waterways as splines, dock points, entrances, depth/occlusion values and animation anchors.
- Tile edge geometry remains independent of visual asset layout and must match neighbours on all touching edges.

## Limitations — not a finished animation system
- The clean plate is an artist-assisted **mask and content reconstruction**, not a newly painted empty scene. Residual tiny distant figures or locally softened detail may remain and need manual retouching. Do not call the image historically validated.
- The two moving boats still include any passengers painted *inside the boat cut-out*; those figures travel with the vessel, but their gestures are not separately animated yet.
- Walkers are painting-matched cut-outs with subtle motion, not full frame-by-frame walking cycles. The bridge and foliage occlusion masks are approximations requiring manual inspection.
- The demo routes are fixed to the starter painting. Newly placed tiles do **not** yet extend those paths; a dynamic street/canal navigation graph is the next implementation milestone.
- Buildings and foliage are not fully separable for wind deformation, only for occlusion and drifting effects.
- Teahouse-specific activities and scoring still need implementation.

## Next steps
1. **Artwork review:** inspect clean plate at 200–400% and retouch any remaining figures and repaired backgrounds, especially the bridge and breakfast house. Adjust occlusion masks until boats disappear only behind masonry.
2. **Full painterly motion sprites:** commission/draw additional walking frames, rowing/poling cycles, tea gestures and independent willow branches in the same style.
3. **Connected-city graph:** map physical road/water arcs, ports at tile centres, entrances, stalls and destinations. Update the graph as tiles are laid and have boats/people follow newly connected routes.
4. **Reusable layered tile assets:** create topology-specific illustrated clean plates and alpha layers before expanding to the full 60–72-tile deck.
5. **Scoring:** six people per player, activity completion, scoring and token return with tests.
