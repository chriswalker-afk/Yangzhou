Acceptance review

Inspect the original painting at 1× and 3×. The test distinguishes a topologically legal join from a visually convincing join. Passing the first does not establish the second.

Geometry and seam checklist

North edge is land; no external street.
East edge is a street port.
South edge is land; no external street.
West edge is a street port.
Street visibly reaches the same 44/220 width at both external edges, without a synthetic patch or obvious crop.
Painted paving and neighbouring boundary details join convincingly in both directions, at 1× and 3×.
Architectural and horticultural details have passed the historical plausibility review.

Exact border and seam approval: pending human inspection.

Artwork quality rubric

0–3 per category. Gate: at least 16 / 18 and every category at least 2. An absent image cannot be approved.
