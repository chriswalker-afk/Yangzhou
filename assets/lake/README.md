# Slender West Lake — retained artwork benchmark

These six **original 512px square illustrated lake references** are the strongest existing detailed visual benchmark. They are shown as the six fixed tiles in the V2 **composition study**, but are **not production-approved**: the exterior canal mouths, bridge-walkway access, natural shoreline continuity and period/historical detail still require independent visual review.

They must **never** be cut, recomposed, stretched, mirrored, blurred or stamped into new placeable expansion tiles. The previous artwork generation and crop-based pipelines have been removed from V2 and remain accessible through Git history.

The game design preserves a 3×2 fixed starter with eight exterior land connections and two north/south navigable water connections. Its exact geometry is in `tile-topology.js`; the new eight tile blueprints and their 32 painting slots are in `assets/v2/manifest.json`.
