Inspect the joins

These are the same square assets and edge masks used by the game—not a panorama or a separate illustration.

Drag to pan, scroll or use the magnification slider to zoom. At high magnification, check whether banks, paving and buildings continue naturally across the joins.

Acceptance contract

All tile edges are centred at world coordinate 110. Roads use 44 world units; water uses 88; landscape has no port. Streets join streets, water joins water, and landscape joins landscape. Buildings remain upright when tiles are rotated.

Passing edge-pixel tests is necessary but not sufficient. Look for duplicated scenery, abrupt transitions, displaced bridges, unnatural banks and buildings obstructing connections.
