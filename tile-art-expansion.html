A real playable 2 × 2 arrangement: north–south stone street, enclosed garden, a turning lane and an existing east–west painted street. The corner and garden remain labelled studies until completely new artwork is created.

All internal edge types are checked from the canonical N/E/S/W topology. A legal road network does not, by itself, prove painterly seam continuity. No art is rotated sideways.

Artwork acceptance

The new street must have a centred 44/220-wide stone road on its north and south edges (20% of its square, X≈502–752 at 1254px). The street corner needs that same width on the north and east; the garden has four land edges; the canal will need centred 88/220-wide water on its north and south (40%, X≈376–878 at 1254px).

All images need visual review at 1× and 3× before they become production masters. Buildings, people and roofs must remain compass-upright. Do not use cropped artwork, blur collars, rotating pictures or generated overlays to disguise mismatched edges.
